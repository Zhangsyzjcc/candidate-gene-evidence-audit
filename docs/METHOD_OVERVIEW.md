# Method overview

The audit separates discovery from eligible external validation, evaluates each
evidence layer independently, and retains negative or discordant findings.

Statuses include `REPLICATED_ALL_EXTERNAL`, `PARTIALLY_REPLICATED`,
`DIRECTIONAL_SUPPORT`, `CONTRADICTED`, `NOT_REPLICATED`,
`EXPLORATORY_NO_EXTERNAL_TEST`, and prespecified `STOPPED_*` states.

Replication is reserved for external testing of the same estimand. Cross-modal
triangulation cannot rescue failed replication. Evidence grades bound the claim
that may be reported and do not establish causality or biological mechanism.
